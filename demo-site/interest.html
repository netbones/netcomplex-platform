About the Gardening Club

Welcome to the Soralia Village Gardening Club! Whether you're a seasoned horticulturist or just starting your green journey, this is the place to share tips, exchange plants, and grow together. We focus on sustainable practices, indigenous plants, and creating beautiful, thriving gardens in our community.
