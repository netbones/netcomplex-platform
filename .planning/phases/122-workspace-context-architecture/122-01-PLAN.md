| T-122-01  | Information Disclosure | WORKSPACE_REGISTRY (global static)        | low      | accept      | Registry contains only workspace-type vocabulary (labels, icon keys, action labels) — zero tenant PII or row data; leakage across tenants is impossible for static metadata.
