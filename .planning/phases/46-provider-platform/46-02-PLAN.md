# Plan 02: Provider Registration with Legal Adherence & Verification

## Objective

Enable third-party providers to register themselves through a controlled self-service flow that includes **tenant-level registration mode configuration**, **legal adherence and terms acceptance**, **due diligence verification**, and **initial verification status assignment** (probation by default). This creates a secure, compliant, and trust-based provider onboarding process.

## must_haves

- [ ] Tenant can configure whether provider registration is open or invitation-only
- [ ] Provider registration includes legal terms acceptance (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Code of Conduct)
- [ ] Provider registration triggers due diligence verification workflow
- [ ] New providers are assigned PROBATION status by default with a credit score threshold for verification
- [ ] Provider registration form validates required fields via Zod schema
- [ ] Duplicate detection prevents registrations with existing company names (per-tenant)
- [ ] Registration creates a `ServiceProvider` record and initial `ProviderVerification` record (status: PROBATION)

## Background

Currently, `ServiceProvider` records are likely created through admin flows or seed data. This plan introduces a comprehensive self-service registration with legal compliance, trust verification, and credit-based progression. The `Tenant` configuration allows admins to control whether registration is open or invitation-only.

**Key decision from research (Assumption A3):** `CommunityServiceListing.providerId` equals `users.id` (the provider's user ID), NOT `serviceProviders.id`. Dashboard queries must filter by `session.user.id`.

## Risks

- **Moderation vs auto-approval:** Must confirm whether `isActive` defaults to true or false. This affects UX and admin workflow.
- **Duplicate company names:** Case-insensitive matching prevents accidental duplicates but may be overly strict for franchises.
- **Email uniqueness:** Should a provider's email be unique per tenant? Currently schema may not enforce this.
- **Legal compliance audit:** Legal agreements must be reviewed by legal team before deployment.
