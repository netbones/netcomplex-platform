### Correction: `@api/*` (366) is NOT a config collapse

Earlier I characterized `@api/server|client|shared` (366 of 463) as a
one-line config collapse. **That was wrong.** Steiger's
`no-public-api-sidestep` has **no path-allow-list** option — only severity and
per-file `files`+`rules` blocks. Worse, its `isIndex` check only recognizes
files whose name starts with `index` (`index.ts`, `index.server.ts`), so the
named sub-barrels `server.ts` / `client.ts` / `shared.ts` are **never** treated
as valid segment public APIs. `@api/server` (→ `src/shared/api/server.ts`) is
therefore always flagged, by design of the rule — not a misconfiguration.

Disposition of the 366:

- They are **intentional ADR-024 server-only sub-barrels** (`@api/server` etc.
  already the established pattern). Silencing them requires either a **custom
  Steiger rule** (ADR-020's "statically enforceable via future custom Steiger
  rule") or accepting them at `warn`.
- Renaming `server.ts` → `index.server.ts` would make Steiger recognize it as
  an index, BUT breaks the `@api/server` tsconfig path alias (which points at
  `server.ts`). Not worth it.
- **Conclusion:** keep `no-public-api-sidestep` at `warn`; the 366 are a known,
  documented limitation, not actionable debt. The real remediation backlog is
  the ~84 genuine deep imports (excluding `@api/*` and the test allow-lists).
