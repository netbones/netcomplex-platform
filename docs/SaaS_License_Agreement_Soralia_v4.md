The following lifecycle rules apply to individual user accounts upon a Departure Event:
• Graceful Checkout: where a user (HOA Member or Resident) gives notice of departure to the HOA Administrator, the Administrator initiates offboarding via the Platform. The user's Platform Address and associated User Data enter the Archival Period (90 days read-only, non-active) immediately upon offboarding.
• Involuntary Departure: where a user vacates without notice, or the HOA Administrator determines that access should be revoked, the Administrator initiates offboarding. The same 90-day Archival Period applies.
• Portability Window (Premium Seats only): prior to the commencement of the Archival Period, a departing Premium Seat holder has thirty (30) days to request a full export of their User Data and message history in a standard machine-readable format, at no charge. The Provider shall process such requests within five (5) business days.
• Archival and Deletion: at the conclusion of the Archival Period, all User Data associated with the departed user shall be permanently and securely deleted from the Platform, unless retention is required by applicable law.
• Unit Address Reissue (Standard Seats): upon a new Household's arrival, the HOA Administrator may reissue the unit-scoped Platform Address within the Community Domain (e.g. unitNNN@[community-domain]) to the incoming household without restriction.
• Personal Address Cooling-Off (Premium Seats): a personal Platform Address within the Community Domain (e.g. myname@[community-domain]) associated with a departed Premium Seat holder may not be reissued to any other user for a period of six (6) months following the expiry of the Archival Period (the Cooling-Off Period). After the Cooling-Off Period, the HOA Administrator may reissue the address at their discretion.

The HOA Administrator is solely responsible for initiating all Departure Events and Arrival provisioning. The Provider shall provide administrative tools within the Platform to facilitate these workflows.
