**RECITALS.**

A. Netbones Solutions Pty Ltd (\"Provider\") has developed and operates
the Soralia Village Directory Platform, a cloud-based community
management and directory software service.

B. The Soralia Village Homeowners Association (\"Client\") is a duly
constituted homeowners association responsible for the governance and
administration of the Soralia Village community, comprising between 100
and 200 households.

Members (owners) and Residents (including tenants/renters) within the
Soralia Village community, and the Provider is willing to grant such a
license on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

**2. LICENSE GRANT**

**2.1 Grant of License**

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement and timely payment
of all Fees, the Provider hereby grants the Client a limited,
non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license to access and
use the Platform during the Term solely for the Client\'s internal
administrative and community management purposes within the Soralia
Village development.

**2.2 Authorized Users and Seat Types**

The Client is authorized to provision the following seat types on the
Platform:

- **Standard Seat** --- one (1) per Household, assigned to the primary
  household login. The Standard Seat holder manages the Household\'s
  Platform Address, Directory Profile, and up to five (5) Address
  Aliases on behalf of individual Household members.

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```

- **Premium Seat** --- an optional named individual login with a
  personal Platform Address within the Community Domain (e.g.
  myname@\[community-domain\]), independent of any unit designation.
  Premium Seats may be held by HOA Members, Residents, or authorized
  community representatives.

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```

- Complimentary Allocation --- five (5) Premium Seats provided at no
  charge to the Client for allocation to HOA board members and
  committee representatives, as designated by the Client from time to
  time.

A Standard Seat is not transferable between Households. A Premium Seat
is associated with a named individual and travels with that individual
if they move between units within the community. Seat sharing is not
permitted.

**2.3 Licence Capacity and Address Aliases**

The initial Licence Count is set out in Schedule A and is based on the
Client\'s estimated community size of 100 to 200 Households. Each
Household Licence includes:

- one (1) Standard Seat (one authenticated login per Household);

  (e.g. unitNNN@\[community-domain\]);

Address Aliases are managed by the Standard Seat holder and do not

**6. DATA, PRIVACY, AND SECURITY**

**6.1 User Data Ownership**

As between the parties, the Client retains all right, title, and
interest in and to the aggregate User Data for community governance
purposes, consistent with its role as Responsible Party under POPIA.
However, each data subject (HOA Member or Resident) retains inherent
ownership rights over their personal information, including the right to
control, access, correct, delete, port, and monetize it, in alignment
with the 31st Human Right to legal ownership of inherent human data and
the working principles demonstrated by Brazil's LGPD Art. 17 and dWallet
pilot. The Provider is granted a limited license to process User Data
solely as Operator to deliver the Services and facilitate data-subject
monetization. The Provider shall implement self-service dWallet tools
enabling data subjects to exercise these rights directly.

**6.2 Data Protection**

Each party shall comply with all applicable data protection and privacy
laws and regulations in connection with this Agreement, including but
not limited to the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013
(POPIA) and any amendments thereto. The Provider shall act as an
\"operator\" (as defined under POPIA) with respect to personal
information processed on behalf of the Client.

**6.3 Personal Information of Residents**

The parties acknowledge that the Platform may process personal

The Provider shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and

**6.6 Data Breach Notification**

The Provider shall notify the Client without undue delay (and in any

**6.7 User Departure and Arrival Flow**

The following lifecycle rules apply to individual user accounts upon a
Departure Event:

- Graceful Checkout: where a user (HOA Member or Resident) gives
  notice of departure to the HOA Administrator, the Administrator
  initiates offboarding via the Platform. The user\'s Platform Address
  and associated User Data enter the Archival Period (90 days
  read-only, non-active) immediately upon offboarding.

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```

- Involuntary Departure: where a user vacates without notice, or the
  HOA Administrator determines that access should be revoked, the
  Administrator initiates offboarding. The same 90-day Archival Period
  applies.

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```

- Portability Window (Premium Seats only): prior to the commencement
  of the Archival Period, a departing Premium Seat holder has
  thirty (30) days to request a full export of their User Data and
  message history in a standard machine-readable format, at no charge.
  The Provider shall process such requests within five (5) business
  days.

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```

- Archival and Deletion: at the conclusion of the Archival Period, all
  User Data associated with the departed user shall be permanently and
  securely deleted from the Platform, unless retention is required by
  applicable law.

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```

- Unit Address Reissue (Standard Seats): upon a new Household\'s
  arrival, the HOA Administrator may reissue the unit-scoped Platform
  Address within the Community Domain (e.g.
  unitNNN@\[community-domain\]) to the incoming household without
  restriction.

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```

- Personal Address Cooling-Off (Premium Seats): a personal Platform
  Address within the Community Domain (e.g.
  myname@\[community-domain\]) associated with a departed Premium Seat
  holder may not be reissued to any other user for a period of six (6)
  months following the expiry of the Archival Period (the Cooling-Off
  Period). After the Cooling-Off Period, the HOA Administrator may
  reissue the address at their discretion.

The HOA Administrator is solely responsible for initiating all Departure
Events and Arrival provisioning. The Provider shall provide
administrative tools within the Platform to facilitate these workflows.

> (c) export or delete their data at any time (subject to legal

> (d) opt out of any monetization stream with immediate effect.\
> Participation is voluntary and opt-in. The Provider shall maintain an
> auditable ledger of consents, rewards, and disbursements. All dWallet
