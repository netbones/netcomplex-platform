# NetComplex — Brand Identity

---

## Brand Essence

**What we are:** The connective tissue of community living.

NetComplex is not a management tool. We are an informatics platform — a living network that gives homeowners associations and their residents a shared language, a common space, and the infrastructure to act together. Where other platforms manage properties, we activate communities.

**Core belief:** A neighbourhood is not just an address. It is a network of relationships, resources, and shared stakes. NetComplex makes that network visible, useful, and participatory.

---

## Positioning Statement

> _For HOAs and their communities who need more than a billing portal, NetComplex is a community informatics platform that replaces fragmented communication with a unified, resident-driven ecosystem — because people don't just live in buildings, they live among each other._

---

## Brand Name

**NetComplex** is a deliberate compound. _Net_ evokes network, interconnection, and digital infrastructure. _Complex_ captures both the residential complex and the sophisticated, layered nature of community life. Together they suggest a structured system designed for human complexity.

**Usage:** Always written as one word, capitalised as "NetComplex". Never "Net Complex", "NETCOMPLEX", or "Netcomplex".

---

## Mission

To transform residential communities from managed populations into informed, engaged, and self-expressive networks.

## Vision

A world where every resident — whether they own or rent — has a meaningful voice, a trusted directory, and the tools to contribute to the place they call home.

## Values

**Openness** — Information flows freely within communities. Residents are not passive recipients; they are contributors.

**Trust** — Every interaction on NetComplex is anchored in verified identity and community accountability.

**Participation** — Engagement is not incidental. We build features that invite it: campaigns, interest groups, surveys, polling, and rewards.

**Transparency** — We don't collect payments or issue bills. Our role is neutral, infrastructural, and community-first.

**Inclusivity** — Value flows to both home owners and occupants. The platform serves residents, not just asset holders.

---

## Brand Personality

NetComplex is:

**Civic, not corporate.** We speak the language of community boards and town squares, not shareholder reports.

**Warm, not cold.** Technology in service of human connection — not the other way around.

**Empowering, not paternalistic.** We give communities tools; they decide how to use them.

**Modern, not flashy.** Clean, considered, and purposeful. We earn trust through clarity.

**Intelligent, not complicated.** The platform is sophisticated under the hood. The experience should feel effortless.

## Voice & Tone

**Voice** is consistent — who NetComplex is. **Tone** shifts by context.

### Voice principles

- Speak plainly. Avoid jargon. If a word serves no purpose, remove it.
- Use active sentences. "Residents post updates" not "Updates are posted by residents."
- Be specific. "Manage maintenance requests" not "streamline operations."
- Be human. Acknowledge that community living is personal.

### Tone by context

| Context                 | Tone                              |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Onboarding              | Welcoming, confident, clear       |
| Feature explanations    | Informative, practical            |
| Community announcements | Warm, inclusive                   |
| Error messages          | Calm, helpful, never apologetic   |
| Marketing               | Purposeful, slightly aspirational |
| Rewards / Web3 features | Enthusiastic, but grounded        |

### Words we use

connect, participate, contribute, directory, community, residents, network, informatics, trusted, transparent, local, activate

### Words we avoid

manage, tenants, units, billing, collect, control, monitor, track (when referring to people)

## Visual Identity

### Logo Concept

The NetComplex mark is built on the idea of **nodes in a network** — points of presence connected by shared infrastructure. The primary mark combines a compact geometric symbol (a network node cluster rendered as an interconnected hexagonal grid fragment) with a wordmark in a geometric sans-serif.

**Symbol:** Three or four interlocking nodes — visually suggesting a neighbourhood, a network, and a diagram simultaneously. The nodes do not form a perfect shape; one is always slightly offset, reflecting the organic, imperfect reality of community.

**Wordmark:** Set in a clean geometric sans with slightly wider tracking. "Net" in primary weight, "Complex" in light or regular weight, creating a visual hierarchy within the single compound word.

**Clear space:** Minimum clear space equal to the height of the capital N in the wordmark on all sides.

### Iconography

Icons follow a **single-weight line style** — 1.5px stroke, rounded caps and joins, 24×24px grid. No filled icons in primary UI (fills are reserved for active/selected states only).

Icon categories map to platform modules:

- **Directory** — person in a dotted circle (the node-in-network motif)
- **Marketplace** — two overlapping tags
- **Maintenance** — wrench with a small checkmark
- **Content / CMS** — a pen inside a grid cell
- **Maps** — pin with a network radiating outward
- **Forms** — a page with connective lines
- **Ticketing** — a perforated stub
- **Surveys & Polling** — bar chart with one bar highlighted
- **Rewards / Web3** — a hexagon with a small star inside
- **Interest Groups** — overlapping circles (Venn-adjacent)
- **Campaigns** — a megaphone aimed at dots (people)
- **Trusted Providers** — a shield with a checkmark

### Photography & Imagery

**Subject matter:** Real residents in real spaces. Communal areas, notice boards, shared gardens, rooftop gatherings, repair work in progress, community markets, diverse age groups talking.

**Avoid:** Stock-photo handshakes, faceless silhouettes, abstract tech imagery, server rooms, glowing interfaces.

**Style:** Natural light. Slightly warm colour grade consistent with the Fieldstone / Canopy palette. Depth of field used to foreground people, not architecture.

**Illustration style (for empty states and onboarding):** Flat geometric line art using the brand palette. Community scenes: paths, trees, building outlines, clusters of people-shaped nodes.

## Tier Architecture

NetComplex offers three tiers,

### FOUNDATION

_For emerging HOAs and smaller complexes just getting started._

Entry-level access to core informatics: residents directory, community announcements, basic CMS, maintenance request filing, and community maps. Ideal for complexes establishing their first digital presence.

**Tone in marketing:** Approachable, encouraging, low-friction. "Start connecting in minutes."

---

### DEPTH

_For active communities ready to deepen engagement._

Everything in FOUNDATION, plus: internal marketplace, interest groups, internal surveys, polling, forms, ticketing, campaigns and the trusted providers module, payments gateway. This is where community informatics becomes community participation.

**Tone in marketing:** Confident, active, community-forward. "Your community, fully alive."

---

### CORE

_For large, high-activity complexes that need the full ecosystem._

Everything in DEPTH, plus: agent hub, external survey rewards, education gateway, Web3, full user content library, and advanced analytics. The complete NetComplex platform.

**Tone in marketing:** Authoritative, expansive, future-looking. "The complete community operating system."

## Brand Applications

### In-product

- The platform UI uses Fieldstone as the page background, Canopy for primary navigation and CTAs.
- Each module has its icon and a consistent header pattern.
- Tier indicators use their respective tier colours — never mixed.
- Rewards and Web3 elements use Grove Gold exclusively.

### Marketing & Communications

- Headlines are large, confident, and brief. One idea per headline.
- Every piece of marketing leads with community — not software.
- Case studies feature real community outcomes: a petition that worked, a provider that was found, a neighbour who was helped.

### Email

- From name: "NetComplex Community" (never just "NetComplex" — always include the human word "community").
- Subject lines: plain language, no emoji, no ALL CAPS.
- Body: Söhne or system sans fallback, 16px, Bark on Fieldstone background.

### Social

- Profile image: symbol mark only (the node cluster) on Canopy background.
- Content pillars: community stories, product updates, resident tips, local HOA news.
- Voice: civic and warm — like a trusted local institution, not a SaaS startup.
