About Our Conservation Area

The Soralia Fynbos Cape Strandveldt Conservation Area encompasses 15 hectares of pristine South African fynbos and wetland ecosystem, representing one of the world's most biodiverse floral kingdoms.

This unique landscape is home to over 150 endemic plant species, including rare proteas, ericas, and restios that are found nowhere else on Earth. The wetland component provides critical habitat for amphibians, reptiles, and numerous bird species.

Did You Know?

The Cape Floral Kingdom, despite covering less than 4% of southern Africa, contains nearly 20% of the continent's flora - more plant species than the entire British Isles!

Species Spotlight

King Protea

Protea cynaroides

South Africa's national flower, this magnificent protea produces large, crown-like flower heads and is a key species in our fynbos ecosystem.

Cape River Frog

Amietia fuscigula

Endemic to the Western Cape, these frogs are indicators of healthy wetland ecosystems and can be heard calling during our rainy season.

Cape Sugarbird

Promerops cafer

This endemic bird is perfectly adapted to feed on protea nectar and plays a crucial role in pollinating our fynbos flowers.
